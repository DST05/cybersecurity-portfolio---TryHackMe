# Computer Types

## Overview

This room introduced the different types of computers used in both everyday life and professional environments. It explored how each type is designed for a specific purpose, considering factors such as performance, cost, portability, and functionality.

---

## Learning Objectives

- Identify different types of computers used in everyday life.
- Distinguish between personal devices, such as laptops and smartphones.
- Recognise computers used indirectly, including servers, IoT devices, and embedded systems.
- Understand the purpose of each type of computer.
- Explain why different computer types are designed for specific tasks and environments.

---

## Key Concepts

Different computer types are designed to meet different requirements. Depending on their intended purpose, factors such as processing power, portability, storage capacity, cost, and energy efficiency influence their design.

### Examples

- **Laptop** – Portable computer designed for general-purpose computing.
- **Smartphone** – Mobile computing device combining communication and application functionality.
- **Server** – Provides services and resources to other computers across a network.
- **Workstation** – High-performance computer designed for demanding professional workloads.
- **IoT Device** – Internet-connected device designed to perform a specific function.
- **Embedded System** – Dedicated computer system designed to perform a single task, such as controlling a washing machine or coffee machine.

## Practical Activities

- Explored the characteristics of different computer types.
- Compared how each type is designed for a specific purpose.
- Identified examples of common computing devices used in personal and enterprise environments.

## Key Takeaways

- Different computer types are designed to fulfil different requirements.
- Performance, portability, cost, and functionality influence computer design.
- Servers, IoT devices, and embedded systems all play important roles within modern computing environments.
- Understanding computer types provides a foundation for learning cybersecurity.

---

## Reflection

Much of the content reinforced knowledge I had previously learned at GCSE level. The main new concepts for me were workstations and the role of servers as specialised computer systems.

This room also demonstrated that cybersecurity extends beyond protecting desktop computers. Security professionals must understand how servers, smartphones, IoT devices, embedded systems, and other computing devices operate because each presents different security risks and requires different protection strategies.
